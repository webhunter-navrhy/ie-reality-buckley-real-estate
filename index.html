Description

Buckley Real Estate are delighted to present Apt 5, 22 Railway Road to the sales market. This impressive 2 bedroom apartment extends to 86 sq. m. and offers bright, spacious accommodation finished to a modern standard throughout. The property features a particularly generous open-plan kitchen, living and dining area, with access to a private balcony. Two well-proportioned double bedrooms, a contemporary bathroom, separate utility area and excellent storage complete the accommodation.

The apartment's modern finish and generous proportions make it an ideal choice for owner-occupiers and investors alike, as there is no rent cap. The open-plan living space provides an excellent setting for both everyday living and entertaining, while the large windows and balcony provide plenty of natural light.

Situated in Baldoyle, the property enjoys a highly convenient coastal location with a wide range of shops, cafés, schools and recreational amenities nearby. Bayside and Clongriffin DART stations provide convenient access to Dublin City Centre, while the area also benefits from excellent bus and road connections. The nearby coastline, Baldoyle Estuary and Sutton provide an excellent selection of walking and leisure opportunities. Dublin Airport is also within a 20 minute drive.

Between size, condition and location, we expect this to be a popular listing. Early viewing is advised,
